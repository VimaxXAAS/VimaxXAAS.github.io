Research Focus

Deciphering grape domestication history, stress resilience mechanisms, and fruit quality using multi-omics pipelines.

Grape Genomics & Domestication

Haplotype-resolved genome assembly, resequencing, and exploration of Xinjiang local germplasm evolutionary history.

Biotic Stress & Defense

Pathogen interaction pathways, rootstock-mediated gray mold resistance, and molecular responses to powdery mildew.

Multi-Omics & Berry Quality

Untargeted metabolomics and transcriptomics investigating berry cracking, sugar profiles, and antioxidant potential.

Candidate Gene Mining

Characterization of Trihelix, Dirigent, Laccase, and BPC transcription factor families for climate-smart breeding.
